Understand your CPF. Plan your future. All offline, all private.

CPF Fortune Cat is an iOS app that helps Singapore CPF members check their monthly contributions, project their retirement savings, and estimate CPF LIFE payouts — entirely offline, with your data stored only on your device.

Features

  • Basic — Monthly Contribution Check
  • Reference — Official CPF Rates & Info
  • Intermediate — Annual Summary
  • Advanced — Month-by-Month Contribution Engine
  • Retirement — CPF LIFE Payout Estimates
  • Projection — Savings Projection to Age 55
  • Appendix — Salary History Tracker

Why CPF Fortune Cat

  • 100% offline calculator — your financial data never leaves your device
  • No advertisements
  • Rates updated annually per CPF Board's official announcements
  • Optional Face ID / Passcode app lock for members

CPF Fortune Cat 是一款帮助新加坡公积金会员查看每月供款、推算退休储蓄、估算 CPF LIFE 月领金额的 iOS 应用——完全离线运作，您的数据只存储在您自己的设备上。

功能

  • Basic — 每月供款速查
  • Reference — 官方公积金费率及信息
  • Intermediate — 年度汇总
  • Advanced — 逐月供款引擎
  • Retirement — CPF LIFE 月领估算
  • Projection — 推算至 55 岁的储蓄预测
  • Appendix — 薪资记录追踪

为什么选择 CPF Fortune Cat

  • 100% 离线计算器——您的财务数据不会离开您的设备
  • 无广告
  • 费率每年根据中央公积金局最新官方公布进行更新
  • 会员可选开启面容 ID / 密码应用锁
